# Sioux Falls Concrete design

Subject: a concrete contractor in Sioux Falls. Look comes from the job itself: cured slab gray, saw cut joints, and the pink red of Sioux quartzite, the local stone.

## Color
- Slab #ECEBE7: page background, like cured concrete
- Paper #F7F6F3: alternate bands and panels
- Ink #1D2226: text and dark bands
- Steel #545B62: secondary text
- Line #CFCCC5: hairlines and joint rules
- Quartz #A33B46 (deep #7F2A34, light #F2B6BB on dark): call buttons, links, marks

## Type
Archivo variable (self hosted, OFL). Headings use the width axis at 112 to 120 percent and weight 800 for a wide, poured feel. Body is 100 percent width, 17px, line height 1.6.

## Layout
Left aligned. Hairline lists instead of card grids. One thin "joint rule" divider under each page header. The signature piece is the sidewalk cross section drawing built from the City standard (components/SlabSection.astro). Photos are hotlinked Unsplash stock (see src/content/photos.ts).

## Rules
- Short sentences, common words, no dashes.
- Every local fact links to its source (src/content/sources.ts).
- No invented reviews, licenses, years, prices, or stats.
- Phone number lives only in src/config/site.ts.
