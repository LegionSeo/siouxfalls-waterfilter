# Town and local fact sources

Checked Nov 20, 2026. Town facts match the sources used for the Sioux Falls Electricians site.

## Brandon
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandon,_South_Dakota : Minnehaha County, five miles east of Sioux Falls, 2020 population 11,048, ZIP 57005, Big Sioux River and Split Rock Creek, Brandon Valley School District includes schools in eastern Sioux Falls.
- https://cityofbrandon.org/inspections : Planning and Inspection Department, 304 Main Ave.

## Harrisburg
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrisburg,_South_Dakota : Lincoln County, about 2.7 miles from the southern edge of Sioux Falls, 2020 population 6,732, Census Bureau 2024 estimate 10,203, ZIP 57032, Harrisburg School District with schools inside Sioux Falls.
- https://dot.sd.gov/media/18a17956/17-089ReportFINAL_07.30.18.pdf : I-29 Exit 71 at 273rd Street.
- https://dot.sd.gov/media/533e33d0/Northern%20Lincoln%20County%20Corridors%20Study_Corridor%20Plan_Final_20240404.pdf : 273rd Street is the main east and west road into Harrisburg.

## Tea
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tea,_South_Dakota : Lincoln County, laid out in 1894, 2020 population 5,598, Census Bureau 2024 estimate 7,699, ZIP 57064, Tea Area School District.
- https://dot.sd.gov/media/18a17956/17-089ReportFINAL_07.30.18.pdf : I-29 Exit 73 at 271st Street.

## Hartford
- https://www.hartfordsd.us/welcome : about 10 miles west of Sioux Falls, West Central schools.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartford,_South_Dakota : Minnehaha County, named Oaksville in 1878, renamed Hartford in 1881, 2020 population 3,354, ZIP 57033.
- https://dot.sd.gov/media/qrppocbx/final-study-report.pdf : SD 38 runs through Hartford and meets I-90 at Exit 390 just east of town.

## Dell Rapids
- https://dot.sd.gov/media/079ac1ff/DellRapids_MTP_Final.pdf : northern Minnehaha County, about 15 miles north of Sioux Falls on the I-29 corridor, Big Sioux River, downtown about three miles from I-29 via SD 115.
- https://www.dr-k12.org/ : Dell Rapids School District.

## Weather
- https://www.weather.gov/fsd/quickref : Sioux Falls record low 42 below zero (Feb 9, 1899), January record low 38 below (1912), record high 110 (Jul 17, 1936 and Jun 21, 1988).

## Permits and licenses (City of Sioux Falls)
- https://www.siouxfalls.gov/business-permits/permits-licenses-inspections/licensing/contractor-licensing/mechanical-contractor : a license is required for all contractors doing mechanical or HVAC work in city limits; owner occupants of single family homes can get homeowner permits instead.
- https://www.siouxfalls.gov/business-permits/permits-licenses-inspections/permits/building-permits/building-permit-faqs : homeowners doing their own work need separate mechanical permits.
