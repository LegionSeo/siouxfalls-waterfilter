# Sioux Falls Water Filtration: design system

Calm, clean and a little premium. Deep water navy with glacier mint.
Keep it clearly different from Sioux Falls Water Filters (cream and sun, drawn house art, pill call buttons in yellow).

## Colors

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| paper | #f3f9fb | Page background |
| mist | #e6f2f6 | Light section bands and panels |
| white | #ffffff | Cards and the form |
| ink | #0b2a3c | Dark hero, footer, headings, body text |
| night | #123a50 | Raised panels on dark sections |
| lagoon | #0a7185 | Links and icons on light and dark |
| aqua | #8fe3ec | Small highlights on dark sections |
| mint | #4fe0bd | Call buttons only |
| mint-deep | #2fc7a3 | Hover for mint |
| muted | #46606e | Secondary copy |
| line | #d5e5ea | Borders and dividers |

Mint means "call". Keep it off large areas.

## Type

- Inter in three weights: 400 body, 600 labels, 800 headings.
- Body 17px, line height 1.65.
- Eyebrows sit above each H1 and H2 in small caps style.

## Shape and spacing

- Buttons are 12px radius rectangles with a 2px border, 48px tall minimum.
- Cards 16px radius, form fields 12px radius.
- Photo panels use a navy shade gradient so white text keeps 4.5:1 contrast.

## Signature pieces

- Full bleed photo hero with a dark left fade. The home H1 stays under 15ch so it never covers the art.
- Compact trust row under the hero buttons: three short items with lagoon icons.
- Split photo panels for city water versus well water.
- Service pages under /services and town pages under /locations, each with an answer box first, then what is included, signs and FAQs.
- Quote form on the home and contact pages.

## Call buttons

- One main action on every screen: Call. Mint fill, ink text, ink border.
- Second action on dark sections is a white outline button (Request service).
- Phones get a sticky bottom bar: Call (wide, mint) plus Text or Email outline buttons.

## Copy

Short sentences, common words, no dashes. Never invent reviews, licenses, years or stats.
