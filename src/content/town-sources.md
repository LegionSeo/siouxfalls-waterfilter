# Sources for local water facts

Town history, population, and housing counts on the town pages carry over from the census and Wikipedia sources used for the original town research. Photo credits are listed in `town-places.ts` and shown on each town page.

## Sioux Falls hardness

- City of Sioux Falls water quality report, 2025 data: https://www.siouxfalls.gov/resident-services/utilities-billing/water/water-quality/water-report
- The "Water Softener Setting, total hardness (GPG)" row lists 15 for Sioux Falls and 10 for Lewis & Clark. Total hardness is 250 and 168 ppm.
- The same report says no health based drinking water violations were recorded in 2025.

## Not confirmed

The water supplier for Harrisburg, Tea, Hartford, Dell Rapids, Canton, and Lennox was not confirmed from a primary source. Those pages tell readers to check their bill and annual report instead of naming a supplier. Add the supplier only after confirming it.
